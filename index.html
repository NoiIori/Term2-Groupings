STVEP

The Strengthened Technical-Vocational Education Program (STVEP)
provides students with practical skills and knowledge that can help them prepare for
future careers. It focuses on hands-on learning, technical skills, and developing students'
abilities for employment, entrepreneurship, and further education.

This webpage does not contain any confidential information.
