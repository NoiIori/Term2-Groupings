WHAT IS SPTVE?

SPTVE is a program of the Department of Education that strengthens technical and vocational education in public secondary schools.

It focuses on helping learners gain practical skills, digital knowledge, and real-world competencies that can be used for further studies and future careers.

HISTORY OF THE PROGRAM

The program in Iloilo National High School has gone through three stages: SOF, STVEP, and SPTVE.

School of the Future (SOF)

It started as the School of the Future (SOF) in 1996–1997, introducing technology-based learning.

STVEP

It later became STVEP, focusing on skills training and career preparation.

SPTVE

Today, it is SPTVE, which continues skills-based education for real-world opportunities.

GOALS OF THE SCHOOL

SPTVE aims to develop learners who are skilled, confident, and ready for real-world challenges.

Its goals include improving critical thinking, technical skills, creativity, teamwork, responsibility, and preparation for future careers or higher education.

MAJORS AND SPECIALIZATIONS

SPTVE combines academic learning with hands-on skills to prepare students for real-life applications in different fields.

Programming

Students learn coding, logic development, and software creation skills used in real-world technology systems.

Information and Communication Fundamentals (ICF)

Focuses on digital tools, ICT systems, and understanding how information technology is used in communication and work.

Entrepreneurship

Develops skills in business planning, managing small enterprises, and understanding how to turn ideas into income opportunities.

Computer Systems Servicing (CSS)

Trains students in computer repair, maintenance, troubleshooting, and basic IT support skills.

Technical Drawing

Improves accuracy in design, visualization, and technical illustration used in engineering and design fields.

Research

Builds skills in writing, data analysis, investigation, and presenting academic outputs clearly.
